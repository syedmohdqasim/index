I am a PhD candidate in Computer Engineering at Boston University, advised by Prof. Ayşe K. Coskun and co-advised by Prof. Gianluca Stringhini. I work at the intersection of distributed systems and machine learning: making large-scale cloud platforms easier to observe, debug, and keep reliable, using ML-based anomaly detection and LLM agents that reason over distributed traces.

Most recently, I built DSTBench (IC2E 2026), a benchmark for evaluating LLM agents on trace-driven performance debugging in microservices, in collaboration with researchers at Google. Earlier work includes online probabilistic tracing (Best Paper Award, IC2E 2024) and a production ML anomaly detection deployment on Chameleon Cloud's OpenStack services (IC2E 2025). This summer I interned at Uber, improving the precision of alert-triggered automated rollbacks for deployments.

Before my PhD, I spent five years in industry — leading platform engineering at State Street and building enterprise microservices and blockchain systems at Thoughtworks.
